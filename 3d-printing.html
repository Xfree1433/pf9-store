What we do

Whether you have a print-ready file or just an idea, we can take it from there.

Design & CAD

Turn a sketch, sample, or spec into a manufacturable 3D model — or refine a file you already have.

Rapid prototyping

Iterate fast. Print a revision, check the fit, adjust, and reprint — before committing to a production run.

Small-batch production

Need a dozen, or a few hundred? We print repeatable batches with consistent settings and QC.

Functional parts

Brackets, mounts, jigs, fixtures, enclosures, and replacement parts — printed in durable PETG.

In development

A common rail and accessory interface for organizing industrial and workstation setups — a shared mounting standard for cups, cradles, brackets, and tool holders. We're validating the design, fit, and load capacity now.
